# Documents you need before taking money (have counsel review all of them)

This is a checklist, not legal advice. None of these exist in the repo as final text.

1. **Terms of Service**: scope of the service, plans and limits, acceptable use (no testing systems you do not own), payment and cancellation, liability cap, governing law.
2. **Privacy policy**: what the public pages and the app store (leads, event counters, workspace data), purposes, retention, rights, contact.
3. **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** for business customers: roles, instructions, sub-processor list (see /trust), security measures (see docs/SECURITY-QUESTIONNAIRE.md), breach notice, deletion and export, audits.
4. **Authorization-test terms**: the customer attests they own or may test the target; no destructive probes; results are advisory, not a certification.
5. **Sub-processor list** kept in step with your real deployment.
6. **Cookie notice**: the public pages set no cookies and use first-party counters only; re-check if you add tracking.
7. **Imprint / company details** where required in your country.
